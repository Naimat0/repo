Extraction & Characterisation of Plant-Based Biosurfactants for Wastewater Treatment

Master's Thesis · Jan 2023–Apr 2024 · Guide: Prof. Suparna Mukherji, ESED, IIT Bombay · Graded AA (10 CGPA)

Investigates Aloe vera waste and Reetha (Sapindus mukorossi) as sustainable alternatives to synthetic surfactants for surfactant-enhanced bioremediation of PAH-contaminated wastewater. Biosurfactants were extracted, characterised and tested for solubilisation of petroleum hydrocarbons. Presented at the National Environment Conference, IIT Bombay.

Solvent extraction · Surface tension · Emulsification index (E24) · UV–Vis · FTIR · HPLC · LC/MS

Metaproteomics Analysis of COVID-19 Patients

Jan 2021–Feb 2022 · Guide: Prof. Sanjeeva Srivastava, BSBE, IIT Bombay · Letter of Recommendation

Analysed mass spectrometry data across 5,000+ peptide sequences from nasopharyngeal swabs to identify potential microbial co-infections, and reviewed 70+ articles on secondary infections in COVID-19 patients.

What I Hope to Explore Through a PhD

I am seeking doctoral research where I can investigate water sustainability and environmental policy through an interdisciplinary lens:

  • How can water resources be managed more sustainably under increasing environmental and societal pressures?
  • How can scientific evidence better inform water policy and institutional decision-making?
  • What enables effective implementation of water sustainability policies?
  • How can technological interventions and nature-based solutions be integrated into water-management strategies?
  • How do institutions and stakeholders shape water governance and sustainability outcomes?
  • How can water sustainability be evaluated using environmental, social and policy indicators?
  • How can the Water–Energy–Food Nexus inform more integrated resource-management policies?

I remain open to refining these questions based on the strengths and ongoing work of the research group I join.
