About

Where Science Meets Sustainability

I am an environmental researcher and sustainability educator with a Master's in Environmental Science & Engineering from IIT Bombay (CPI 9.49, Department Rank 1) and a Double Gold Medal in Botany from Banaras Hindu University (CPI 9.47, Rank 1/110).

My academic journey began with a simple question: how can scientific knowledge be transformed into solutions that work in the real world? My master's thesis focused on plant-derived biosurfactants for wastewater bioremediation. Between lab work, I have monitored river water quality in the field and built GHG accounting tools for industrial clients.

Currently, I am a Senior Associate in Training & Education at the IIT Bombay Green Energy & Sustainability Hub (GESH IITB), where I lead the Living Lab Fellowship Program and a campus-scale sustainability project in collaboration with the Sustainability Office of IIT Bombay.

My path runs through a state water resources department, consultancy firms and a university sustainability hub, and each stop has sharpened the same question: how do we get science translated into policy and infrastructure that actually gets built, funded, and maintained?
